How I work

First I find what sticks out most. Only then do I connect everything else.

I first see every project as a whole, from a distance. Then I go into the key building blocks one by one, finding what wasn't visible from afar. In the end I know what depends on what and in which order we go. That's also what you get from me.

  1. The whole picture first

    Before anything is built, I draw on one page what the problem is made of. No details yet, but every part.

    You geta one-page map of the problem

  2. Key building blocks

    I find the points that stick out most: what has to hold for everything else to make sense. With Gobe, it was safety.

    You getbuilding blocks with priority and reasons

  3. Links and prerequisites

    Only then do I connect them: what depends on what, what affects what, what can run in parallel and what can't.

    You getthe order of work and the critical path

  4. Blind spots

    As the work goes on, I add what couldn't be seen from afar, and for each finding I write down what it changes.

    You getrisks, each with a decision

  5. A documented map

    In the end every decision, reason and trade-off is written down. The team knows what it's doing and why, even after I've left.

    You getdocumentation the team keeps using
