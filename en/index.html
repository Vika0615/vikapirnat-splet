How I think

What you can expect from me

  1. The whole picture first

    I don't look at single features but at the whole product, the customers and the business. I look for the biggest lever.

  2. Evidence over assumptions

    I understand the problem before I look for a solution. I combine data, the voice of users and what people in the company know.

  3. Order out of chaos

    I turn fragmented information into a clear picture and decisions a team can act on.

  4. AI with judgement

    I use AI for research, analysis, learning and building. I know when to trust it, when to check it and when a person decides.
