How I work

What you can expect from me

  • The whole picture first

    I don't look at single features only. I want to understand the whole product, the customers and the business, and where the biggest lever is.

  • Evidence over assumptions

    I understand the problem before looking for a solution, combining data, users and what people in the company know.

  • Order out of chaos

    I turn fragmented, unclear information into a clear picture and decisions a team can act on.

  • I learn fast

    I enjoy stepping into new or complex domains. I break them into parts and keep refining my picture of them.
