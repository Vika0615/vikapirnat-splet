How I work

The whole picture first. Then what everything rests on.

I'm available for a single piece of work or a longer engagement. The approach stays the same; only the scope changes.

Approach

Four steps

  1. The whole picture first

    I draw on one page what the problem is made of.

    You getone view the whole team understands

  2. What has to hold

    I find what everything rests on. With Gobe, it was safety.

    You getclarity on what matters most and why

  3. The order of work

    What depends on what, what can run in parallel and what can't.

    You getwhat comes first and what can wait

  4. Written down as I go

    Blind spots, decisions, reasons and trade-offs.

    You geta team that knows what it's building and why, even after I've left

What I help with

When to call me

  • You have an idea, but don't know if anyone will use it.

    From idea to first version

    Who it's for, mock-ups, a working first version of one main flow with analytics, and a test with real people.

  • The product exists, but people drop off somewhere.

    Product reviews

    Where they get stuck, what to fix first and what to measure.

  • Development is under way, but nobody knows what matters most.

    Roadmap planning

    What comes first, what later and what not at all, with a reason for each decision.

  • You're planning a new feature.

    Feature reviews

    Who it's for, what it must do and how you'll know it works.

  • Your team wants to work with AI but doesn't know where to start.

    AI and processes in small companies

    Where AI saves time and where it doesn't. How to set up digital and team processes around it.

  • You're building, but have no product manager.

    Fractional product manager

    Product management for a team that has no capacity for it.

AI in my work

I don't always deliver faster. I deliver better.

I've been using AI in my work for two years. It's a big part of my job as a product manager, but it doesn't replace judgement.

  • Research and synthesis

    With ChatGPT and Claude I review the market, sources and edge cases. I check every source myself.

  • A knowledge base in Obsidian

    Notes, decisions and the project log live in one place. AI works from my notes, not from memory.

  • Prototypes with Claude Code

    I set the plan, the order and the rules. AI writes the code; I review every step.

  • Judgement

    I know when to trust AI, when to check it and when a person decides.
