Problem

There's an idea, but nobody to test it

You have an idea for a digital product, or a product that isn't working, but nobody to find out who it's for, what it must do and what it mustn't, and whether people use it. Without that, you build on guesswork.

What I help with

I'm available for

  • Product reviews

    I look at your product through your users' eyes: where people get stuck, what to fix first and what to measure.

  • Roadmap planning

    Priorities and a plan: what we build first, what comes later and what we don't build, with a reason for each decision.

  • Feature reviews

    Before you build a new feature: who it's for, what it must do, and how you'll know it works.

  • Idea validation

    Who the product is for and whether people actually need it: user types, mock-ups, a test with real people.

  • Measurement

    What to measure so you can see what people actually do. Consent-based analytics, in line with GDPR.

  • Fractional product manager

    For a team that's building but has no capacity for product management.
