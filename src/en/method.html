How I work

The whole picture first. Then what everything rests on.

I'm available for a single piece of work or a longer engagement. The approach stays the same; only the scope changes.

Approach

From the whole picture to the order of work

  1. The whole picture first

    I draw on one page what the problem is made of.

    You geta picture the whole team understands

  2. The foundation

    I find what has to hold for everything else to make sense. With Gobe, it was safety.

    You getclarity on what matters most and why

  3. The order of work

    I work out what depends on what and what can run in parallel.

    You getwhat comes first and what can wait

  4. Written down as I go

    I write down blind spots, decisions, reasons and trade-offs as they come up.

    You geta team that knows what it's building and why, even after I've left

What I help with

When to call me

  • You have an idea, but don't know if anyone will use it.

    From idea to first version

    I find out who it's for. Then mock-ups, a working first version of the main flow with analytics, and a test with real people.

  • The product exists, but people drop off somewhere.

    Product reviews

    I find where they get stuck, what to fix first and what to measure.

  • Development is under way, but nobody knows what matters most.

    Roadmap planning

    A plan: what comes first, what later and what not at all. With a reason for each decision.

  • You're planning a new feature.

    Feature reviews

    I check who it's for, what it must do and how you'll know it works.

  • You want to bring in AI or build something new, but don't know where to start.

    AI and digital innovation

    I find where AI saves time and where it doesn't. I help develop digital innovations and set up the digital and team processes around them.

  • You're building, but have no product manager.

    Fractional product manager

    I take on product management in your team: priorities, decisions and alignment.

AI in my work

I don't always deliver faster. I deliver better.

I've been using AI in my work for two years, and it's a big part of what I do as a product manager. It doesn't replace judgement.

  • Research and synthesis

    With ChatGPT and Claude I review the market, sources and edge cases. I check every source myself.

  • A knowledge base

    I keep notes, decisions and the project log in Obsidian. AI works from them, so it doesn't guess.

  • Prototypes with Claude Code

    The plan, the order and the rules are mine. AI writes the code; I review every step.

  • Judgement

    AI shows the options. Which one is right, I decide.
